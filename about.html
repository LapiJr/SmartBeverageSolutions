About us

Fresh cakes, good coffee and a calm place

Vienna Konditorei is a small cafe on the Plaats, in the centre of The Hague. This is what we are about.

Cakes and pastries

Baked fresh every day

Our cakes and pastries are made fresh. A warm croissant in the morning, a slice of apple pie in the afternoon, or a Bossche bol when you want a real treat.

Ask us about the cake of the day.

Coffee and food

Good coffee, breakfast and lunch

We take our time with every cup, from a short espresso to a smooth flat white. Start your day with a French Start or a granola pot, or come by for lunch with a sandwich on sourdough, a warm panini or a fresh salad.

See the menu

The place

A quiet spot in a busy city

Outside, the city moves fast. Inside, we keep things calm and cosy. Our team is friendly and happy to help, in English or in Dutch.

On sunny days our terrace looks out over the Hofvijver. The cafe is on the ground floor and wheelchair accessible.
