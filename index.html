Welcome

A calm place in the city centre

Vienna Konditorei is a small cafe in the heart of The Hague. We bake fresh cakes and pastries, make good coffee and serve breakfast and lunch every day.

Inside it is warm and quiet, with a friendly face behind the counter. On sunny days you can sit on our terrace with a view over the Hofvijver.
