Privacy

Privacy statement

Draft. This privacy statement is a draft. The owner of Vienna Konditorei should check it, and ideally have it reviewed, before it is final.

Vienna Konditorei respects your privacy. In this statement we explain in plain language which personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and how long we keep it. We follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, in Dutch: AVG).

Who we are

Vienna Konditorei, Plaats 30, 2513 AE Den Haag, the Netherlands. Phone: 06 14728081.

Contact form

When you send us a message, we receive your name, your email address and your message. We use this only to answer you. We keep these messages for up to 12 months, unless we need them longer to finish handling your question.

Job applications

When you apply for a job, we receive your name, email address, phone number, the role you choose, your availability, the languages you speak, your motivation and your CV, if you add one. We use this only to assess your application and to contact you about it.

  • If you are not hired, we delete your details no later than 4 weeks after the application process has ended.
  • With your permission, we may keep your details for up to 1 year, in case a suitable job opens up. You can withdraw this permission at any time.
  • If you are hired, your details become part of your staff file.

Who can see your data

Only the owner and the manager of Vienna Konditorei can read your messages and applications. Our website and its forms are hosted by Netlify, which processes the data on our behalf. Netlify may store data outside the European Union, under the protection of standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

Cookies and other services

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We remember your language choice (English or Dutch) in your own browser. The map on our website is provided by Google Maps and the fonts by Google Fonts. When these load, Google may receive your IP address. Google's own privacy policy applies to this.

Your rights

You have the right to see, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to how we use it. Just call us or send us a message through the contact form. We will reply within 4 weeks. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security

We take care to protect your data. Our website uses a secure connection (HTTPS), and only the people who need your details can see them.

Changes

We may update this statement from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
